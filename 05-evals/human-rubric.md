## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno needs to accurately turn signals into problems and jobs-to-be-done.
- **Value proposition:** Juno will accurately categorize problems and jobs-to-be-done and extract the signal source.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy in derived statement | Does the problem statement or job-to-be-done accurately reflect the signal it was derived form? | Yes / No | The AI written statement does not reflect the input signal. Also if Juno makes up or implies any information.  | n/a | The AI written statement is a reflection of the input signal. No information is made up or implied. |
| Accuracy in signal source | Is the assigned signal author accurate? | Yes / No | Signal author is not accurate. | n/a | Signal author is accurate.  |
| Accuracy categorizing  | Is the problem statement or job-to-be-done categorized accurately? | Yes / No | No the category is not appropriate.  | n/a | Yes the assigned category is appropriate.  |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 10 cards a month
- **Who scores them with you:** Another PM scores them with me.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If we disagree in how a card is scored. We will discuss and bring in more team members until we have agreement.

## Pass bar

To start an accuracy rate of 7 out of 10, or a 70% accuracy.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- No
